Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • MS Net Speed

1. Overview & Commitment

MS Net Speed ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing real-time network speed monitoring while protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled when you use the MS Net Speed Android application.

2. Information We Process

MS Net Speed operates entirely on your Android device. We process only technical network traffic counters required to compute live speed:

  • Network Byte Counters: Transmitted (TX) and received (RX) byte totals are queried locally via standard Android TrafficStats APIs to calculate real-time download and upload speeds.
  • Active Connection Type: Network interface status is queried locally via ConnectivityManager to identify Wi-Fi, Cellular, Hotspot, or Ethernet connections.

3. Zero Personal Data Collection & Guarantee

We strictly do NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Specifically:

  • No browsing history, URLs, or web search queries are tracked.
  • No IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers are recorded.
  • No personal data is ever shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. System Permissions Explanation

To deliver real-time speed monitoring, MS Net Speed requests the following standard Android permissions:

  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to detect active connection types (Wi-Fi, Mobile, Hotspot).
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to maintain a background service to compute speed every second without interruption.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Optional permission used only if you enable the Floating Speed Overlay to draw speed text directly in the status bar area.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to automatically restart the speed monitoring service when your device powers on.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with Android OS updates and Google Play Developer Policies. Updated policies will be posted on this page.
